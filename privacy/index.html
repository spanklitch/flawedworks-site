Website privacy

Last updated: August 2026

Apps and this website are different surfaces. Our iOS apps are built local-first — no ads and no in-app analytics from Flawedworks. This page covers only flawedworks.com (the marketing site). For each product, see ZAGNALS privacy and Mebit privacy.

What this site is

flawedworks.com is a static marketing site for Flawedworks products (and links to sibling projects such as Verqury). It is not an app backend and does not store your app data.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Netlify. The domain’s DNS and edge services may run through Cloudflare. Those providers may collect standard operational metrics (for example request counts and security logs) needed to host and protect the site.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, delivered through Cloudflare Zaraz (edge tag management). This helps us understand aggregate traffic: how many people visit, which pages they open, and which links they use. We use it to improve the site and Gary’s technical portfolio presence — not to sell data and not to run advertising on these pages.

We do not put analytics SDKs inside ZAGNALS or Mebit. Visiting this website does not make those apps send usage data to us.

What we do not do

  • No selling of personal information
  • No advertising networks on this marketing site
  • No combining website analytics with private app content (notes, displays, etc.)

When you contact us

If you email us (for example info@flawedworks.com), we see what you send so we can reply. That is separate from site analytics.

Your choices

You can limit analytics cookies and similar storage in your browser settings. Blocking third-party scripts may reduce or stop GA4 collection for your visits; the site content still works without analytics.
