Privacy Policy

Effective: March 2026 · Last updated: 13 August 2026

ZAGNALS collects no data of any kind. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. ZAGNALS never transmits anything, stores nothing remotely, and shares nothing with the developer or any third party. The one exception worth naming is not the app sending data anywhere, but Apple’s own Siri: if you choose to control ZAGNALS by voice, that request is handled by Siri under Apple’s privacy policy. See Siri and Shortcuts.

What data ZAGNALS collects

None. ZAGNALS does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal information or usage data.

How ZAGNALS stores your settings

ZAGNALS saves your preferences (display duration, text color, timer settings, etc.) locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults system, in a container shared with the ZAGNALS widget so the widget can show your current countdown. This data:

  • Exists only on your device
  • Is never transmitted over a network
  • Is never accessible to the developer or any third party
  • Can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app

Network access

ZAGNALS has no network access of any kind. It does not connect to the internet, does not make API calls, and does not communicate with any server — at any time, for any reason.

Siri and Shortcuts

ZAGNALS can be controlled by voice — “Start ZAGNALS countdown” and similar — and appears in Apple’s Shortcuts app so you can build your own automations. This is built on Apple’s App Intents, and it is optional: ZAGNALS works exactly the same if you never use it.

When you speak to Siri, Siri handles that request, not ZAGNALS. What you say goes to Apple and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, the same as any other Siri request on your iPhone. ZAGNALS receives only the resulting command — start, pause, resume, restart — and never the audio. Nothing about it reaches FlawedWorks, and the app still makes no network connection of its own.

Notifications

ZAGNALS schedules local notifications so a countdown can still reach you when the app is not on screen, and marks them time-sensitive so they are not held back by Focus. These are created and delivered entirely by your device. No notification content is sent to us or to anyone else, and no push server is involved — ZAGNALS has no remote push capability at all.

Third-party SDKs and analytics

ZAGNALS contains zero third-party SDKs, frameworks, or libraries. There are no analytics tools, no crash reporters, no advertising networks, and no tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Because ZAGNALS collects no data from anyone, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and is safe for users of all ages.

Advertising

ZAGNALS contains no advertising and never will.

This marketing website (flawedworks.com)

This policy is about the ZAGNALS app. The Flawedworks marketing site is a separate surface: it uses privacy-conscious web analytics (Google Analytics 4 via Cloudflare Zaraz) so we can understand aggregate traffic to product pages. That does not change anything in the app — ZAGNALS still has no network access and sends no data. Full website details: flawedworks.com website privacy.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Because the app collects no data, any changes would only ever be clarifications — not expansions of data collection in the app.
