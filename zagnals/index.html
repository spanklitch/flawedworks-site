Four displays. Zero distractions.

ZAGNALS fills the screen with exactly what the room needs to see — nothing more.

  • CUE — Full-screen message board. Type your cue, pick white or amber text, set a duration. Save up to 10 favorite messages for one-tap reuse. One tap launches it full-screen. App Store →
  • Clock — Large-format clock readable across a room. 12h or 24h, three color modes: dark, light, or alternating.
  • Countdown — Precision countdown timer with configurable audio, flash, and haptic alerts. Show Clock overlay. App Store (classroom) →
  • Speech Timer — Full-screen green/yellow/red “traffic light” for public speaking. Presets or custom green/yellow/red times, count up or down, with G/Y/R letters for colorblind clarity. App Store →

New in 1.3: the Speech Timer.

Turn any iPhone or iPad into a full-screen speech “traffic light.” As you speak, the screen moves from green (you’ve hit your minimum) to yellow (your target) to red (time’s up) — with a bold G / Y / R letter alongside every color, so the signal is unmistakable even for colorblind viewers.

  • One-tap presets, or set your own green / yellow / red times in seconds
  • Count up from zero or down to your limit
  • Optional milestone beep and an at-a-glance session report
  • Made for public-speaking practice, club meetings, debates, and contests
