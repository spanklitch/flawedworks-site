Say it all at once.
Mebit sorts it out.

You don't think in tidy single items, so you shouldn't have to speak that way. Say everything on your mind in one breath — Mebit splits it into separate notes.

"We're out of dog food, I need to call the dentist Tuesday, and Kim's birthday is September 2nd"
becomes three notes
Out of dog food Call the dentist Tuesday Kim's birthday September 2

Fix it while it's fresh

The transcript appears the moment you stop talking. Misheard a name? Tap it and type, or just say it again — right there, while you still remember what you meant.

It offers. You decide.

Say "Tuesday at three" and the reminder is set. Something next spring goes to your calendar with a tap. Mention a store and Mebit can nudge you when you're actually there. Ignore any of it and nothing breaks.

Your workspace

Not a wall of notes.
Five shelves.

A list of everything you've ever said is just a pile, and a pile is paralysing. Mebit sorts each note by what you meant to do with it, then shows you five lines — so you can open the one you're ready for and leave the rest alone.
