Get Mebit — free
1

Install Mebit from the App Store, then open it once so it can set up voice.

2

Add the control. Swipe down for Control Center, tap +, and add Errand Mode — or start it from Settings, or say “Errand mode in Mebit.”

3

Head out. Arrive at a store and Mebit tells you what you needed there. It ends itself after two hours, or stop it anytime.
