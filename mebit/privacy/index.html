Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Mebit is an iOS app made by FlawedWorks, an independent software studio. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data in the app. The short version: your notes live on your device, and nothing leaves it unless you choose to share or use an AI provider you configure yourself. We have no app servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics in the app.

What we collect (the app)

Nothing from the app. FlawedWorks operates no app backend and has no account system for Mebit. We never receive, store, or see your notes, your voice, your location, or your in-app usage. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking of any kind in the app.

Your notes ("bits")

The notes you capture are stored locally on your device in an on-device database. They remain there unless you explicitly share a circle (see Sharing below). Deleting a bit removes it from your active view; deletions propagate to anyone you've shared a circle with.

Voice capture

Speech-to-text runs entirely on your device using on-device models. Your audio is never uploaded to any server — not ours, not Apple's, not anyone's. The microphone is used only while you are actively capturing a note.

AI enrichment and queries

Mebit can tag your notes and answer your questions using AI. You choose how:

  • Apple Intelligence (on supported devices) runs on-device. Your text does not leave your phone.
  • Your own AI provider key (Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google) sends the relevant note text and your question to that provider under your own account/key, subject to that provider's privacy policy. FlawedWorks never sees, routes, or stores any of it. Your key is held in the device Keychain.
  • No AI — capture-only mode works with no provider configured.

Sharing (circles)

If you invite someone to a circle, the bits in that circle sync to that person through Apple's CloudKit, using your iCloud account. This data travels through Apple's infrastructure under Apple's privacy terms — FlawedWorks has no server in the path and cannot read it. Sharing only happens for circles you explicitly share; your private circle is never shared.

Location

By default, Mebit only reads your location while you are using it. It takes a single reading at the moment you ask a question, so answers can be location-aware, and it can optionally stamp where a note was captured. In this default mode there is no background location use at all, and no location history is built.

Place nudges are optional, and off until you turn them on. If you enable them, Mebit asks iOS for “Always” location access and then does use location in the background — watching for you arriving at a place you have named or mentioned in a note, so it can quietly surface that note. It does this with significant-location changes and geofences around a small number of places at a time.

Even then, all of it happens on your device. Your location is evaluated locally, is never sent to us or anyone else, and no trail of where you have been is stored or transmitted. FlawedWorks has no server in the path and receives no location data whatsoever. You can turn Place nudges off at any time in Settings → Location, which stops the background use immediately.

An optional place label you attach to a note is stored with that note, on your device.

Notifications

Mebit may schedule local reminders (for example, to check whether an old note is still relevant). These are generated and delivered on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Crash diagnostics

Mebit uses Apple’s MetricKit to receive reports about its own crashes and freezes, so problems can be found and fixed. These reports stay on your device and are visible to you at Settings → Help → Diagnostics. They contain technical information only — which part of the app stopped and why — never the content of your notes. Nothing is transmitted to us automatically; we only ever see a report if you choose to send us one yourself.

Children

Mebit is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone via the app.

This marketing website (flawedworks.com)

This policy is about the Mebit app. The Flawedworks marketing site is a separate surface: it uses privacy-conscious web analytics (Google Analytics 4 via Cloudflare Zaraz) so we can understand aggregate traffic to product pages. Visiting the website does not make the app send us your notes or usage. Full website details: flawedworks.com website privacy.
