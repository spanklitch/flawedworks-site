Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Mebit has no account and no sign-up. Your notes live on your device from the moment you install it.

How do I capture and retrieve a note?

Two buttons. Capture — tap and speak (or type); three seconds of silence saves it. Retrieve — ask a question in plain language and Mebit surfaces the answer from your own notes.

Do I have to pay for AI?

No. Smart recall works on any iPhone with zero setup. On supported devices you can add Apple Intelligence (on-device, free), or connect your own AI provider key — Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini — for richer answers. You hold the key; FlawedWorks never sees your queries.

How does sharing work?

You can open a shared space with a partner or family member. Bits in that circle sync through your iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit — shared only with the people you choose. Your private notes are never shared.

Is my data private?

Yes — that's the foundation. Your notes stay on your device, voice transcription is on-device, and there are no ads, no analytics, and no data sold. See the full privacy policy.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you reinstall Mebit or set up a new device, open the app's settings and choose Restore Purchases to recover your one-time Mebit Solo purchase or your Mebit Sharing subscription on the same Apple ID.
